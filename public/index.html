HOW TO PLAY

OFFENSE

  • Tap a play card. Drag the QB to move him.
  • Throw: touch away from the QB, pull back like a slingshot, release. Put the X on a receiver and it locks on, leading him.
  • Your runner follows your finger (grab him and he rides above your thumb). Keep it ahead of him.
  • JUKE just before contact: an untackleable burst, then a stumble.

DEFENSE

  • Pick a coverage: MAN, ZONE or BLITZ.
  • Your defender (yellow ring) follows your finger. Touch the runner to chase him; run into him to tackle.
  • Tap another defender or hit SWITCH to take control of the one nearest the ball.

4TH DOWN

  • Go for it, PUNT, or kick a FIELD GOAL.
  • Field goal: tap KICK when the needle is in the green.
  • TD = 7 pts (auto extra point), FG = 3, safety = 2.
